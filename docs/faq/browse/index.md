# Browse
Frequently Asked Questions about Browse.

## Why can't I see installed sources?

### If the extension language differs from your device's primary language
Enable the source's language at <nav to="sources">, tap on **Filter**, then turn on the language of the desired source.

### If it's an NSFW extension
Every extension is marked **Safe**, **Mixed** or **18+**, and only the kinds you allow are loaded. Go to <nav to="browse"> and check **Allowed content warnings** under **Extensions**; all three are allowed until you change it.

With **Also apply to installed extensions** on, an extension you already installed stays in the list without its sources when its warning is not allowed. Allowing **Mixed** or **18+**, or turning that switch off, asks you to unlock your device first. Changes apply straight away.
